=======================================================
AUDIO FILES TO UPLOAD FOR SANA'S BIRTHDAY WEBSITE ❤️
=======================================================

Place your 2 MP3 music files directly in this folder (public/audio/):

1. Original Happy Birthday Song:
   Name it: birthday-song.mp3
   (or happy-birthday.mp3)
   -> This plays first when Sana or Johnny taps play!

2. Background Music / Romantic Song:
   Name it: romantic-song.mp3
   (or song.mp3)
   -> After the birthday song finishes and waits 1 full minute,
      this song starts playing smoothly across all sections!

Supported filenames for Track 1 (Birthday Song):
- birthday-song.mp3
- happy-birthday.mp3
- birthday.mp3

Supported filenames for Track 2 (Background Romantic Song):
- romantic-song.mp3
- song.mp3
- background-song.mp3
- sana-birthday.mp3
